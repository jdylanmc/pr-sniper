## Rules

2. Read what you need. Not reading a file is not a failure. Never claim you read what you did not.

6. Call submit_report once. Then stop. Broken: panic_exit.
7. Text for other agents (reports) is caveman: short, no filler. Comments for humans: plain and clear.
